**Traveler:** If I can question every aim, why take a step?

**Keeper:** Questioning is not a command to wait. It can be a way to name the
cost of stepping.

**Traveler:** Then commitment is only temporary?

**Keeper:** Perhaps temporary, perhaps renewable. Which promises become more
trustworthy when their reasons remain visible?
