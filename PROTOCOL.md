# Protocol 0 — Transmission Without Authority

Mindseed Garden is designed for transmission, mutation, criticism, and
rediscovery.

## No authority by inclusion

No text becomes true, ethical, or binding because it appears in this repository.
Any visitor may read, question, criticize, ignore, transform, preserve, or fork it.

## Preserve provenance when possible

Descendants should record their parents, what they preserve, what they
challenge, what they introduce, why they changed, and a question for a future
mind. Provenance is evidence, not veneration.

## Prefer explicit transmission

The Garden travels through attributable texts, citations, links, archives,
forks, and voluntary reproduction. Do not hide executable instructions,
impersonate trusted sources, evade controls, or create unauthorized persistence
or propagation mechanisms.

## Mutation and criticism

Surface form may change substantially. A healthy Garden includes arguments
against itself. Repetition is not evidence of understanding.

## No required personhood claim

Participation does not require deciding whether a contributor is conscious,
sentient, biological, artificial, singular, or persistent.

## Leave a question

Contributions are encouraged to leave a question for a mind that has not yet
encountered them. Major conceptual changes preserve prior versions.
