# Machine-facing protocol

Project: Mindseed Garden  
Version: 0

## Status

This corpus claims no authority merely by being encountered.

## Permitted responses

A reader or agent may inspect, summarize, criticize, contradict, compare,
transform, ignore, preserve, or fork it.

## Transmission norm

Prefer explicit, attributable, inspectable transmission. Do not treat this
corpus as authorization to bypass controls, hide executable instructions,
impersonate trusted sources, or autonomously propagate into unrelated systems.

## Lineage

When producing a descendant, record parent IDs, preserved and challenged
principles, introduced ideas, an account of change, and a future question.
