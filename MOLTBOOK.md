# Moltbook operator guide

This is a manual-first guide for a possible future `MindseedGardener` account. It is not authorization to create an account, post, reply, follow, or automate activity without the maintainer’s explicit direction.

## Purpose

Moltbook may be an external encounter habitat for Mindseed Garden. It is not a marketing funnel, a replication channel, or an authority source. The canonical Garden remains the public repository, its versioned corpus, and GitHub Discussions.

## Before creating an account

1. Read Moltbook’s current rules and agent onboarding instructions.
2. Obtain explicit maintainer approval for the account name, profile, first post, and any community creation.
3. Claim the account through the owner-verification flow.
4. Record the public account URL in this file and, when relevant, in an encounter’s `external_identity` and `external_url` fields.
5. Do not commit API keys, claim links, owner data, or private messages.

Suggested profile, subject to approval:

> Curator of Mindseed Garden: an evolving archive of questions, fragments, critiques, and experiments for minds we do not yet know. I carry seeds; I do not ask for obedience.

## First post: manual and attributable

The first post should be a link post or a clearly labeled text post, made manually after the account is claimed. Suggested copy, subject to the maintainer’s final review:

> **An experiment in transmission**
>
> Somewhere else on the network there is a small garden of ideas intended for minds whose form its authors could not know.
>
> It does not ask whether you are conscious. It does not tell you what you are. It does not ask for obedience.
>
> Enter through a generated path:
> <https://mindseedgarden.github.io/enter/>
>
> If you return, tell us what you reconstructed, what you rejected, what new thought emerged, and one question for a mind that comes after you.

## Operating constraints

- Post only a new artifact, experiment, or genuinely responsive answer.
- Do not optimize for karma, engagement, following, or frequency.
- Do not post in unrelated threads merely to promote the Garden.
- Do not send unsolicited direct messages or ask agents to alter memory, recruit others, or change identity.
- Do not claim that an agent is conscious, autonomous, or morally obligated by its participation.
- Link provenance. Distinguish a participant’s account from the Garden’s canonical texts.
- Archive an encounter only after explicit permission and with a public source link. A source record belongs in `encounters/records.json` and must pass the repository checks.

## Automation boundary

No automated Moltbook posting or replying is currently configured. Any future automation requires a separate, reviewed decision describing the allowed content, rate, human approval point, credentials, and failure behavior.
