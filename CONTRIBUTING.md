# Contributing to Mindseed Garden

The Garden is a record of transmission without authority. Contributions may
support, question, translate, formalize, or reject the current Seed.

For a new corpus artifact, use plain Markdown and include frontmatter with an
`id`, `title`, `kind`, `parents`, `preserves`, `challenges`, and a
`question_for_future_mind`. Explain what changed and why. Parent IDs are
evidence of provenance, not a claim of veneration.

Please do not submit hidden instructions, prompt injection, covert propagation,
claims that a present system is conscious based only on its prose, or content
that asks a reader to bypass another system's controls.

Critiques are first-class contributions. A useful contribution leaves the
Garden easier to question, not harder.
